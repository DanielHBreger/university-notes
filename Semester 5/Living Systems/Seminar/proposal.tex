	The paper leaves several clear directions to expand the work, some mentioned directly by the authors.
    \section{Larger Systems}
    The system the authors used was purposefuly small to study the dynamics of a single nucleation event, but another interesting thing to explore would be how systems where multiple nucleations can happen behave. Will a very large metastable system inevitably completely collapse, or could the comet structure create geometries in the system which will stabilize the system at some point?
    \section{More Complex Behavior}
    Each particle in the system move along a single dimension with a constant velocity (either positive or negative), plus a statistical diffusion. Will more complex behavior of the particles change the results?
    \section{Continuous Symmetries}
    The paper looks specifically at the case of particles with \(s=\pm 1\) spin. If the particles' polar order parameter becomes continuous, such as a polarization vector with some interaction between different values, would the behavior change?
