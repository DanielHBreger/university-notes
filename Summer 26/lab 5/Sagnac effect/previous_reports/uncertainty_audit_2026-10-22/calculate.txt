5. SETTING MEANS
   Mean of the photos, u = SEM = SD/sqrt(n): 0.004 to 0.028 rad (statistical).
   The rest mean (SEM 0.020 rad) is subtracted from every setting: a common offset, which a line
   with a free intercept absorbs. It enters the slope only as the rest point's own deviation.
   Second half minus first half of each setting: beyond 2 of its photo-noise errors in 8 of 10 settings (2.5-6.0 errors).
   Implied wander of a setting mean: 0.039 +- 0.007 rad (bootstrap over settings),
   so the SEM, which assumes independent photos, understates how well a setting mean is known.

6. REGRESSION Delta phi = a omega + b (ten setting means, equal weights; Schwartz Eq. 7)
   s = sqrt(sum(Delta phi - line)^2 / 8) = 0.0447 rad: the uncertainty of every point, taken from the points.
   u_stat = s / sqrt(sum (omega - mean)^2) = 0.0239; calibration line (4000 redrawn lines) 0.0037; together 0.0242.
   a = 1.8390 -> 1.84 ± 0.02 rad/(rad/s); R^2 = 0.9986.
   With the photos' SEM and eps as weights instead: chi2/dof = 56.6/8, p = 2e-09:
   those error bars are too small for the differences between settings (section 5), hence the regression.
   Against a_th = 1.7381 +- 0.1371: z = 0.72, p = 0.47 (regression error only).
   Distance of each point from the line against the setting's mean fringe period P: r = +0.81, p = 0.005
   (with the median P of Table 2: r = +0.82, p = 0.004); ring-fit and cosine-fit mean periods agree, r = 0.89.
   P per setting, 10^5 px^2: medians 1.058-1.094, means 1.057-1.099.
