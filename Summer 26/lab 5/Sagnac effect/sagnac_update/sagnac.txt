Systematics not in the numbers above:
  * The settings were taken in increasing order (16:21-16:33) with the only rest frames at the start, so a phase drift linear in time is indistinguishable from the Sagnac slope. A rest frame at the end (or the reverse rotation) would test it.
    Fitting phi = a omega + D t + b: a = 2.15 +- 0.20 rad/(rad/s), D = -0.9 +- 0.5 mrad/s, correlation -0.992. The within-setting drifts range over -6.9 to +8.2 mrad/s and the residual scatter about the line is sigma_x = 0.041 rad, so the phase wanders but does not random-walk; a steady drift is not excluded by this.
  * omega is the calibration at the same setting, not measured during the photographs (the reproducibility eps covers the scatter seen in the calibration).
  * The sign of the shift (rings shrink) is not compared: the rotation direction is not recorded here.
