  AIA assumes that only the phase changes between frames. On synthetic profiles (tests/test_methods.py) it recovers a 3.3 rad sweep to 0.01 rad; a 5 % brightness drift leaves the slope right but puts single frames off by up to 0.1 rad, and a 1.7 % frame-to-frame change of the fringe period, as measured, gives errors of order 0.2 rad. Its spread over windows and variants here measures how far the photographs break that assumption.
