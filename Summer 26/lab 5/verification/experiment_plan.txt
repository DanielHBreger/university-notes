M4. Lorenz map
From one long double-scroll record: smoothV1 lightly (Savitzky–Golay, window about1/20of a
period), find the local maxima with a prominence threshold of2 %of the range, and plotM n+1
againstM n. Draw the diagonal.
If the points fall on a curve rather than a cloud, the three-dimensional flow has reduced to a
one-dimensional map — the experimental signature of a horseshoe, and the strongest single result
in the experiment. Repeat for two or three records at differentRand show how the map changes.

Oscilloscope settings — check at the start of every session
1.Channel mapping.CH1=V 1 (node 1), CH2=V 2 (node 2), CH3=V 3 (node 3), as labelled
in Figure 1. These are the defaults assumed byrpot.py. Verify once at the start:V 1 is
the one with the larger swing, several volts, whileV2 stays below a volt. Swapping CH1 and
CH2 makesrpot.pyreturn a resistance outside the range of the potentiometer, which is the
quickest way to notice.
2.DC coupling on all three channels.AC coupling deletes exactly the information that
distinguishes a single scroll from a symmetric orbit.
3.20 MHzbandwidth limit ON.The signal is2.85 kHz; the filter is free.
4.Fix V/div once and leave it.Changing a range changes that channel’s gain by a percent
or two, which is tens of ohms in the divider result.
5.Probe attenuation in the menu must match the probe switch.
6.×10probes, compensatedagainst the Probe Comp square wave.×1probes add about
90 pFto node 1 —0.9 %ofC 1, enough to shift boundaries by a few ohms.
7.Acquisition Normal, not Peak Detect or Average.
8.20 msper division, giving a200 msrecord.
Time budget
Three measurement meetings of six hours.
Meeting A.
Setup and channel check,V–Icurve (M1), fit, and the M2 prediction. Then a first hunt for
the double scroll. Must end with the fitted curve and a written prediction.
Meeting B.
M3 in earnest: both boundaries of the double-scroll range, plus the long records. Start M4 on
the spot, so you know whether the records are good enough before the equipment goes away.
