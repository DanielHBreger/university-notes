\section{Streamlines and pathlines}
\begin{colbox}
	Consider the following 2D flow \begin{equation}
		\vb{v}\ins{x,y} = \mqty(\alpha yt \\ \beta)
	\end{equation}
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item Find the streamlines \(\vb{x}\ins{x,t}\) and the pathlines \(\vb{x}\ins{t,\vb{x}_0}\) of the flow.
		\item Create an animation of the motion of a particle starting at position \((0,0)\) at \(t=0\) and \(\alpha=\beta=1\). In the animation plot you should display the streamlines at different times, as well as the full trajector of the particle. Discuss the relation between the streamlines and the trajectory of the particle.
	\end{enumerate}
\end{colbox}
\subsection{Streamlines and Pathlines}
As we know the streamlines satisfy
\begin{equation}
	\frac{dx_s}{\alpha yt} = \frac{dy_s}{\beta}
\end{equation}
therefore
\begin{equation}
	\dv{x_s}{y_s} = \frac{\alpha yt}{\beta}
\end{equation}
integrating w.r.t y
\begin{equation}
	x_s = \frac{\alpha t}{2\beta}y^2+C
\end{equation}
so the streamlines are
\begin{equation}
	x_s - \frac{\alpha t}{2\beta}y^2=C
\end{equation}
