\section{Newton Method as an iterated map}
\begin{colbox}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Write down the Newton map explicitly for \(g(x) = x^2-4\). Show that it has exactly two fixed points that coincide with the roots of \(g\), and that both are superstable in the sense of the definition above.
        \item Starting from \(x_0=1\) iterate and report the first few iterates \(x_1,x_2,\dots\) until the result agrees with \(x^*=2\) to your tool's precision
        \item Return to a general twice differentiable \(g\) and let \(x^*\) be a root, \(g(x^*)=0\). Show that \(x^*\) is a superstable fixed point of the newton map if \(g'(x^*)\neq0\). What happens if \(g'(x^*)=0\)?
    \end{enumerate}
\end{colbox}
