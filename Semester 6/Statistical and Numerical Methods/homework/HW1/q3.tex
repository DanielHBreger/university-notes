\section{Random Coin}
\begin{colbox}
\(k+1\) coins are arranged in a row so that the \(i\)th coin has a probability of \(\frac{i}{k}\) to fall on heads. A coin is selected from the row at random and is flipped \(n\) times. If all \(n\) flips were heads, what's the chance the \(n+1\)th flip will be heads?
\end{colbox}
